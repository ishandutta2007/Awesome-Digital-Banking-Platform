# Awesome-Digital-Banking-Platform

## Top Digital Banking Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Retail & Business Digital Banking, Core Engagement Layers, Omnichannel Banking UX & Composable Banking Front Ends*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Banking Platforms**. These systems power mobile and online banking experiences—accounts, payments, onboarding, and engagement—often sitting on top of core banking systems.



**Examples** include Backbase, Q2, Amount, Alkami, Sopra Banking Platform, Meniga, Kony DBX, Finastra Digital Banking, Lumin Digital, Tyfone, Mambu, Thought Machine, Temenos Infinity, SDK.finance, ebankIT, and Fiserv Architect (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full retail digital banking UX is largely commercial. Open strength is in **core banking** (Apache Fineract / Mifos), **payments infrastructure** (jPOS, Moov), and composable banking APIs. This section lists every significant relevant project found.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Apache Fineract](https://github.com/apache/fineract)**  

  Leading open-source core banking platform—accounts, loans, savings, and APIs used worldwide (especially with Mifos).



- **[Mifos X / openMF](https://github.com/openMF)**  

  Open financial inclusion platform built on Fineract—web apps, mobile, and payment hub components for digital banking services.



- **[jPOS](https://github.com/jpos/jPOS)**  

  Open-source financial messaging and transaction infrastructure—ISO-8583/20022 gateways used in real payment systems.



- **[Moov](https://github.com/moov-io)**  

  Open-source financial building blocks—ACH, FedNow-oriented tooling, and payment operations components.



- **[Apache Fineract CN / community distributions](https://github.com/apache/fineract)**  

  Cloud-native and community variants extending Fineract for modern deployments.



- **[Open Banking API frameworks](https://github.com/search?q=open+banking+API+PSD2+OR+FAPI)**  

  Community implementations of Open Banking / FAPI patterns for account and payment APIs.



- **[CoreBanking / simple open cores](https://github.com/search?q=core+banking+open+source)**  

  Additional educational and regional open core banking projects (evaluate maturity carefully).



- **[Keycloak + open IAM for banking apps](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak)**  

  Open identity layer frequently used in custom digital banking front ends.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Core ledger**: Apache Fineract + Mifos apps for inclusive and challenger banking backends.

- **Payments rails**: jPOS and Moov for messaging and ACH-class flows.

- **Composable stacks**: Fineract APIs → custom mobile/web UX → open IAM → card/payment processors.

- Commercial digital banking platforms still lead in polished retail UX, regulatory accelerators, and bank IT integration.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

**Apache Fineract** / **Mifos** for core; **jPOS** / **Moov** for payments; custom or open web/mobile for channels.  

Commercial platforms (Backbase, Q2, Alkami, Mambu, Temenos, etc.) provide engagement layers and core modernization paths.  

Fintechs and inclusive finance programs often run Fineract successfully; large retail banks typically buy commercial digital banking platforms. Fully open digital banking is achievable for focused products; full-service retail parity requires significant build effort.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Banking software is highly regulated. Deploy only with appropriate licenses, audits, capital requirements, and security controls. Incorrect money movement causes direct customer harm.

- Open-source cores offer transparency and control but place compliance and operations on the institution. Commercial platforms shift product and often regulatory packaging to the vendor. Neither replaces banking licenses and prudent risk management.
